    Encoding

    Slab height is the bill. Solid fill is money already in the vault, the dashed outline is what's left, and hatching is the projection from the auto-save rule.

    Interaction

    Hover a row and its slab slides out like a drawer. Press E to explode the stack. Drag the sweep history to replay the quarter and watch the vault fill.

    One drawing, three renderings

    Paper, Blueprint and Night are token sets over the same SVG geometry. Switching modes swaps tokens, never markup, so the drawing morphs in place.
