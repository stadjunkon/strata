      Readme. A

      3 notes

      Encoding

      Slab height is the bill. Striped fill is money already in the vault, the dashed outline is what's left, and dotted fill is the projection from the 30% auto-save rule.

      Interaction

      Hover a layer row and its slab slides out like a drawer. Press E to explode the stack, S to run a scan. Drag the sweep history or press Space to replay the quarter; the log fills as payments land.

      Style

      FUI terminal: header bars with teal end caps, tick rulers, striped progress, mono readouts. The iso drawing is generated in JS from one geometry and styled entirely with tokens.
